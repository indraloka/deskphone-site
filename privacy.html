Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Developer: indralokaps ("we", "us") · Contact: indralokaps@gmail.com

1. Summary

deskphone turns your iPhone into a desktop-style workspace on an external display. It works as a web browser with windows, a Dock and shortcuts. We do not run servers that collect your data, we do not have user accounts, and we do not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored locally and is never sent to us:

  • Browsing data: cookies, cache, local storage and login sessions, kept by Apple's WebKit so you stay signed in across windows, like in Safari.
  • Workspace state: open windows (page address, title, scroll position) so your session is restored after the app closes.
  • Settings and shortcuts: your home screen and Dock shortcuts, website icons, per-site zoom, pointer and trackpad settings, screen saver delay and power-saving options.
  • Saved workspaces, browser profiles and locked sites: the layouts and names you save, the separate browser profiles you create (each with its own cookies and logins), and the list of sites you lock, so they are available next time.
  • Device status: your iPhone's battery level, network connection type and volume, read on your device only to show them on the external display. They are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Subscription status: whether deskphone Pro is active, so Pro features open without delay.
  • Diagnostic logs: short technical logs kept by iOS on your device to help fix problems. They are not transmitted to us.

You can clear cached website data from the app menu. Deleting the app removes all data the app stored.

3. Calendar, Reminders and Face ID (optional)

  • Calendar and Reminders. This is an optional deskphone Pro feature and it is off by default. If you turn on "Calendar & Reminder Alerts", iOS asks for your permission. The app then reads the titles and times of your upcoming calendar events and the titles and due times of your reminders, on your device only, to show alert banners on the external display. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else, is not shared with the websites you open, is not used for advertising or analytics, and is not kept after the alert is shown. The app never changes your events or reminders. You can turn the feature off in the app at any time, or withdraw access in iOS Settings > deskphone.
  • Face ID and passcode. If you lock a site with the lock feature, unlocking uses iOS's own authentication (Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode). The app only learns whether authentication succeeded. It never receives or stores your face, fingerprint or passcode data.

4. Information sent to other parties

  • Websites you visit. Pages load directly from the websites you open, exactly as in any browser. Those sites receive your IP address, standard browser information and any cookies they set. deskphone presents a desktop (Mac-style) browser identifier so sites show their desktop version. Each website's own privacy policy applies.
  • Default shortcuts. The shortcuts included with the app open well-known web services, several of which are provided by Google (for example its mail, calendar and file services). Your use of them is governed by those services' own terms and privacy policies.
  • Web search. Text you type in the address bar or Spotlight that is not a web address is opened as a search on Google Search and is therefore sent to Google.
  • Website icons. When you add a shortcut, the app downloads the site's icon directly from that website. No third-party icon service is used.
  • Clipboard. The app reads or writes the clipboard only when you copy or paste in a web page or the address bar.
  • Photos and files. When a website asks you to upload a file, the app shows the iOS photo or file picker. Only the items you choose are shared with that website. The app does not get access to your whole library.
  • Camera, microphone and location. If a website asks to use them (for example a video call or a map), iOS asks for your permission first. The data goes to that website, not to us.

5. Subscriptions

deskphone is free to use with limits, and offers an optional subscription, deskphone Pro, billed monthly or yearly. Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment details and no Apple Account information. The app only checks, on your device, whether your Pro subscription is active. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles payment data.

6. Children

deskphone is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law), you may have rights over personal data. Contact indralokaps@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Data stays on your device, protected by iOS. Data in transit to websites uses the encryption those websites provide.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change, and for material changes we will notify you in the app or on the App Store page.
