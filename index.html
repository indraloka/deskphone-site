Your iPhone, a desktop on any screen.

deskphone turns your iPhone into a trackpad and keyboard for a windowed workspace on a TV or monitor. No laptop needed.

Coming soon to iPhone. One email at launch, nothing else.

Why deskphone

A real workspaceDraggable windows, a Dock, Split View and Spotlight-style search, drawn on the big screen.
iPhone as the controllerTrackpad, keyboard and volume in your hand. The screen shows the workspace, not your phone.
Works over cable or AirPlayUSB-C/HDMI or AirPlay to a TV, projector or monitor. Hotel, classroom or living room.
Private by designNo account and no tracking. Locked windows open with Face ID.

How it works

  1. Connect your iPhone to a screen with a cable or AirPlay.
  2. The workspace appears on the big screen.
  3. Use your iPhone as a trackpad and keyboard to open, move and arrange windows.
Free to start, Pro when you need more

Free includes one window. Pro adds multiple windows, Split View, saved workspaces, browser profiles, Face ID lock and Calendar & Reminder alerts.
